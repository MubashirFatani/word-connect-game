Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how Word Connect App handles your information.

Last Updated: April 2025
🛡️

Overview

Word Connect App is a word search puzzle game designed for entertainment. We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect (if any), how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

Short version: Word Connect App does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information. Your game data stays on your device.
📋

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically, Word Connect App does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact details
  • Location data or GPS information
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Photos, contacts, or any files from your device
  • Financial or payment information
  • Any information shared with third parties
💾

Local Data Storage

Word Connect App stores the following game-related data locally on your device only using Android's SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device:

  • Your coin balance earned within the game
  • Your progress (unlocked levels per difficulty)
  • Remaining hints and skip counts
  • Daily reward claim timestamps
  • First-time play flag (to show the tutorial once)

This data is used solely to provide a consistent and personalised game experience. It is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

🔒

Third-Party Services

Word Connect App currently does not integrate any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, analytics tools, or social login services. We do not share any data with any external parties.

  • No advertisements are displayed in the current version of the app
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs are used
  • No social media logins or sharing features
  • No in-app purchases or payment processors
📢

Advertising (Future Versions)

The current version of Word Connect App does not display any advertisements. However, we reserve the right to introduce advertisements in future versions of the App to support continued development and maintenance.

If advertisements are introduced in a future update, they may be served through third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdMob). These networks may collect certain non-personal device information (such as device type, OS version, and ad interaction data) to serve relevant ads. Any such integration will be disclosed in an updated version of this Privacy Policy.

  • Ads will only be shown in future versions, not in the current release
  • We will update this policy before introducing any ad network
  • We will never sell your personal data to advertisers
  • You will be notified of any major privacy changes through an app update
👶

Children's Privacy

Word Connect App is suitable for all ages, including children under 13. Because we do not collect any personal information at all, the App is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar international regulations. No data is collected from children or any other users.

📱

App Permissions

Word Connect App requests only the minimum necessary permissions to function:

  • Storage (Local only)
    Used to save your game progress and settings locally on your device.
  • Internet (optional)
    May be used in future versions for optional leaderboards or updates. No data is sent currently.
🔐

Data Security

Since we do not transmit any data over the internet, there is no risk of your data being intercepted or accessed by unauthorised parties through our app. All game data remains sandboxed within your device's secure storage.

🔄

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If significant changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
